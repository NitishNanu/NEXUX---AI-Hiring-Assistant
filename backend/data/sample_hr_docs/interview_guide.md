# Interview Preparation Guide — HR Knowledge Base

## Types of Interviews

### 1. Phone Screen
- Duration: 15-30 minutes
- Purpose: Initial fit assessment
- Focus: Background, motivation, salary expectations
- Tip: Have your resume in front of you

### 2. Technical Interview
- Duration: 45-90 minutes
- Purpose: Assess technical competency
- Format: Coding problems, system design, domain questions
- Tip: Think aloud, ask clarifying questions

### 3. Behavioral Interview
- Duration: 30-60 minutes
- Purpose: Assess soft skills and cultural fit
- Method: STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- Tip: Prepare 5-7 stories covering leadership, conflict, failure, teamwork, initiative

### 4. Panel Interview
- Duration: 60-90 minutes
- Purpose: Multiple perspective assessment
- Tip: Make eye contact with all interviewers, address each person

## STAR Method Deep Dive

**Situation:** Set the context (1-2 sentences)
**Task:** Describe your responsibility (1 sentence)
**Action:** Explain what YOU did specifically (2-3 sentences)
**Result:** Share the measurable outcome (1-2 sentences)

**Example:**
- **S:** "Our e-commerce platform was experiencing a 15% cart abandonment rate."
- **T:** "I was tasked with identifying and fixing the root causes."
- **A:** "I analyzed user session recordings, identified 3 UX friction points, redesigned the checkout flow, and A/B tested the changes."
- **R:** "Cart abandonment decreased by 8%, resulting in $500K additional annual revenue."

## Common Interview Questions by Category

### Leadership
- "Tell me about a time you led a team through a difficult project."
- "How do you handle underperforming team members?"

### Problem-Solving
- "Describe a complex technical problem you solved."
- "How do you approach debugging a production issue?"

### Teamwork
- "Tell me about a successful collaboration experience."
- "How do you handle disagreements with colleagues?"

### Failure & Growth
- "Tell me about a time you failed. What did you learn?"
- "Describe a time you received critical feedback."

## Questions to Ask the Interviewer
1. "What does success look like in this role in the first 90 days?"
2. "What are the biggest challenges the team is currently facing?"
3. "How would you describe the team culture?"
4. "What opportunities are there for growth and development?"
5. "What's the tech stack, and are there plans to evolve it?"

## Red Flags to Avoid
- Speaking negatively about previous employers
- Being unprepared (not researching the company)
- Giving vague answers without specific examples
- Not asking any questions
- Appearing disinterested
